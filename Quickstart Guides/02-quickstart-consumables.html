Field Hospital Calculator Suite

Quick Start — Consumables Calculator

Version 1.1.2

1. What this calculator does

This calculator helps you plan consumable supplies for a field hospital deployment. You can start from a built-in ward or intensive care (ICU) supply list, adjust it for your situation, and see estimated quantities. It is intended for clinical, nursing, and logistics staff doing deployment planning — not as a substitute for final procurement rules at your facility.

2. Before you start

  • Approximate length of deployment (how many days you expect to operate).
  • Number of beds you are planning around.
  • Whether you want a safety buffer on top of calculated amounts (as a percentage), and what percentage makes sense for your team.
  • A sense of whether your operation is closer to a general ward or a higher-acuity ICU profile, so you pick the right starting list.
  • Optional: any items you already know you must add or remove for your specific mission.

3. How to use it — step by step

  1. Open the consumables calculator from the suite.
  2. Choose Ward Consumables or ICU Consumables to load a baseline list. This replaces the current worksheet list with that standard list so you can tailor from a full starting point.
  3. Enter deployment parameters: expected length of deployment (days), expected number of beds, and buffer percentage if you use one.
  4. Review the requirements table: each row shows an item and its calculated quantity. Use search or sort if you need to find items or prioritize large quantities.
  5. Remove rows you do not need, or use Add Item to include supplies that are not on the built-in list.
  6. When the plan looks right, save a named scenario or export to a file for backup or sharing (see Saving your work).
  7. Use Print if you need a paper copy of the worksheet view.

4. Understanding the results

The main outputs are the quantities per item in the table, based on your deployment settings and any buffer. Totals and counts reflect what you can see in the table after search or filters — hidden rows are not included in those summaries. Use these numbers to support ordering, packing, and resupply planning. The tool is a simplified estimator; always verify quantities and assumptions for your real-world context.

5. Saving your work

Save a named scenario with Save scenario when you want a version you can reload later in this browser. Use Export to file to keep a backup or send your plan to someone else. If something disappears from the screen, try Restore Autosave on the toolbar to recover the last automatically saved worksheet state.
