Supply & Disposal Mode

  • Select how potable water is supplied and how wastewater is disposed of.
  • Self-supplied: Water delivered to on-site storage; wastewater collected and removed. Standard field hospital model.
  • Mains water: Connected to municipal or base water supply. Enter the mains flow rate to check adequacy against demand.
  • Mains sewer: Connected to municipal or base sewage. No wastewater pickups required.
  • Demand calculations are the same in all modes.
Confirm with site survey or supply authority. Typical field supply: 100–500 Gal/hr (380–1,900 L/hr).
